SECTION 3: COMBINED CHARGING UNIT (CCU) DIAGNOSTICS

3.1 Charging System Fault Identification
When an EV displays charging failure warnings or fails to initiate AC/DC fast charging, follow the CCU diagnostic routine.

3.2 Recommended Checks for Charging System Faults:
1. Charge Socket Actuator & Pin Inspection:
   - Check the mechanical charging cable locking pin actuator M30. Verify locking pin extends and retracts freely without mechanical binding.
   - Inspect AC phase pins L1, L2, L3 and DC pins Pos/Neg for heat discoloration, pitting, or corrosion.

2. CP/PP Signal Line Verification:
   - Measure Control Pilot (CP) signal duty cycle (1kHz PWM signal) and Proximity Pilot (PP) resistance using an oscilloscope.
   - Verify CP voltage levels: 12V (unplugged), 9V (plugged), 6V (charging request).

3. High-Voltage Insulation Test:
   - Perform an insulation resistance test on the CCU high-voltage outputs to chassis ground at 1000V DC. Minimum acceptable resistance is 50 M-Ohm.
