SECTION 2: THERMAL MANAGEMENT SYSTEM CHECKS

2.1 Cooling Circuit Overview
The thermal management system regulates operating temperatures for the High-Voltage Battery, Electric Drive Motor (eDrive), and Power Electronics (EME).

2.2 Thermal Diagnostic & Inspection Steps
For thermal management faults and battery cooling performance issues:
1. Electric Fan Assembly Inspection:
   - Check dual electric radiator fan stages 1 and 2 via ISTA component triggering test.
   - Ensure radiator shroud louvers open fully when fan command exceeds 60% duty cycle.

2. Coolant Mixture & Quality Test:
   - Measure glycol concentration using a refractometer. Specific gravity must correspond to a 50/50 water-glycol protection level down to -37°C.
   - Test for coolant contamination or air pockets. Perform an automated vacuum bleeding procedure using the BMW cooling system filling equipment.

3. Electric Switch Valves:
   - Verify 3-way switch valve Y53 (Motor/Battery Heat Swap Valve) changes position correctly during warmup and cooling modes.
