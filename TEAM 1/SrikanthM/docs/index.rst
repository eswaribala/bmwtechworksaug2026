EV Battery Health Intelligence
==============================

Technical documentation for the EV Battery Health Intelligence project. The
project turns vehicle telemetry, vehicle master data, and charging-session CSVs
into one health record per vehicle, then exposes the result through Amazon S3,
Athena, and QuickSight.
