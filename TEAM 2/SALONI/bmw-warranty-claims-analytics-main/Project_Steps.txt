
Top warranty-cost components:

```sql
SELECT
    COMPONENT,
    COUNT(*) AS CLAIM_COUNT,
    SUM(CLAIM_AMOUNT) AS TOTAL_WARRANTY_COST
FROM BMW_WARRANTY_ANALYTICS.WARRANTY.WARRANTY_CLAIMS
GROUP BY COMPONENT
ORDER BY TOTAL_WARRANTY_COST DESC
LIMIT 3;
```
